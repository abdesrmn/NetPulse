What the numbers mean

  • Download: how fast data reaches you (video, websites).
  • Upload: how fast you send data (calls, photos).
  • Ping: delay in milliseconds. Lower is better.
  • Jitter: how much the ping varies. Lower means a steadier call or game.

For a reliable result

  1. Close streaming and downloads on other devices.
  2. Test on Wi-Fi, then again with a cable.
  3. Test again in the evening to see peak-hour speed.
